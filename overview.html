Checks your inbox every hour for approvals, posts them, and emails you the link. Worst case, about an hour between your reply and the post going live.

What Apps Script actually does

It is the approval reader. It sits inside your Google account, wakes up every hour, looks for replies that start with PUBLISH, and posts them to LinkedIn.

You chose it over the cloud version because Gmail wanted a published privacy policy and terms of service before it would let an external app read your mail. Apps Script is not an external app — it is your own account acting on your own inbox — so none of that applies. No consent screen, no token to renew, no deployment.

You do not run it. Once the hourly trigger exists, it runs on its own whether your laptop is open or not. You only open the editor again if something breaks, or to turn it off.

What you actually do each week

  • Read three draft emails. Reply PUBLISH, rewrite and reply, or ignore.
  • Comment on two or three posts by other people — the agent will suggest who and draft the comments, but you send them. This is the part that grows a network, and it is not automatable through LinkedIn's API.
  • Open the chat agent when you want job matches or a post on something specific.
